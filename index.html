FAQ

Questions we get asked.

What area do you cover?

Laredo, Texas. Send us the address and your dates and we will confirm availability and quote you a flat rate.

What do you need from me to get started?

Just the location, your dates and what you want the board to say. We design and provide the artwork — you approve it before it goes up. If you would rather supply your own files, build them at 380 × 180 pixels and keep the text big; people are reading it from a moving car.

Can the screen play video?

Yes. The board runs static graphics, animation and full video, and it can rotate through several messages or sponsors on a loop.

Is it readable in direct sunlight?

It is built for it. These are high-definition, high-brightness outdoor panels — bright enough to read straight into the sun, and every photo in our gallery was taken in open Laredo daylight. At night the screen dims automatically so it stays comfortable for drivers.

How much room does the trailer need?

Roughly a parking space for the trailer, plus clearance to tow in and swing the outriggers out. Level ground is ideal, but the jacks let us set up on dirt and gravel too.
